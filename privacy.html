Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Blackstone Advanced Technologies ("we," "our," "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our SMS Attendance Reporting Service ("Service").

2. Information We Collect

When you use the Service, we collect the following information:

  • Phone Number – The mobile number you text from, used to identify the conversation session.
  • Employee ID – Your company-assigned employee identification number.
  • Absence Reason – The reason you provide for your absence (e.g., Sick, Late, Emergency, Family Leave).
  • Department – Your department within the company.
  • Timestamp – The date and time your absence report was submitted.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • Recording and tracking employee attendance and absences.
  • Displaying absence reports on the internal company attendance dashboard.
  • Responding to your text message with confirmation of your report.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your information is accessible only to:

  • Authorized company management and HR personnel via the internal attendance dashboard.
  • Twilio, Inc., our SMS service provider, which processes messages on our behalf. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored in our company's internal database hosted on company-managed servers. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Absence records are retained in accordance with company record-keeping policies and applicable labor regulations. Phone numbers used for SMS sessions are not retained beyond the active conversation unless associated with an absence record.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of SMS messages at any time by texting STOP.
  • Request access to your absence records by contacting HR.
  • Request correction of inaccurate records by contacting your supervisor.

8. No Marketing

We will never use your phone number for marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes. The Service is used exclusively for attendance reporting.

9. Children's Privacy

This Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the HR department at Blackstone Advanced Technologies.
